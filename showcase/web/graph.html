Who talks to whom

Each point is a Kubernetes namespace. A line means Calico saw real traffic between two namespaces in the last 30 days, and a thicker line means more flows. Databases, Redis, Vault and mail are drawn as small marks on the namespace that uses them instead of as points of their own. Namespaces with no observed traffic sit in the outer ring, grouped by kind.
